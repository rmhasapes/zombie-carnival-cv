Use iPhone wirelessly (Mac Continuity Camera)

No cable is needed. Before connecting, put the iPhone and Mac on the same Apple Account with two-factor authentication, turn on Wi-Fi and Bluetooth, keep them within 10 m / 30 ft, turn on Continuity Camera in iPhone Settings → General → AirPlay & Continuity, then lock and mount the iPhone in landscape. Choose the iPhone from Camera source and click Connect.

On the first visit, browsers can hide camera names until permission is granted. Click Connect once, allow camera access, click Stop, then select the newly named iPhone camera and click Connect again.

Use HDMI for the projector; wireless Continuity Camera is unavailable while the Mac is using AirPlay or Sidecar. If the iPhone does not appear, unlock and relock it after a restart, then reconnect or temporarily use USB to restore the option.

Reading the live state

SEARCHING: no acceptable ball contour. Check color, crop, area, and shape settings.

TRACKING: the ball is detected but no clear wall approach exists yet.

APPROACHING: apparent size indicates travel toward the wall.

IMPACT: an approaching tracked ball entered or crossed the calibrated wall-size tolerance. Its mapped location is scored as a hit or miss.

IMPACT CANDIDATE: a fallback is waiting for rebound or disappearance evidence.

LOST: the ball has been absent longer than Lost timeout.

Thresholds

Change one value at a time and make several throws. Geometry controls explicitly marked below require repeating the nine radius points.

Quick symptom guide

No outline: resample five ball colors, verify the crop, then adjust Hue tolerance or Minimum area.

Outline but SEARCHING: lower Tracking confidence slightly.

TRACKING but not APPROACHING: verify direction, then lower the approach-speed settings.

Approaching but no impact: use Balanced mode or widen Plane ratio tolerance.

False impacts: use Strict mode, narrow Plane ratio tolerance, or raise contour confidence.

Wrong mapped location: refit the projector first, then remap and validate corners; thresholds cannot correct alignment.
